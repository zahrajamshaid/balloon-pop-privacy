Balloon Pop Privacy Policy

App: Balloon Pop: ABC & 123 Kids (pk.funlearn.balloon_pop_abc) · Last updated 29 September 2026

Short version: the app collects no personal information from children or parents. Stars and settings stay on the device. Ads come from Google AdMob, set to child-directed, G-rated and non-personalised.

What we collect

Nothing personal. The app has no accounts, sign-in, chat, camera, microphone or location access. It does not ask for names, email addresses, photos or contacts.

What stays on the device

Stars earned and the voice and sound settings are saved only on the phone or tablet. They are never sent to us. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob, which takes part in Google Play's Families Self-Certified Ads SDK Program. Every ad request is tagged as directed to children and under the age of consent, limited to G-rated content, and non-personalised. The app does not use the Android Advertising ID. Ads never appear during play.

To deliver ads and prevent fraud, Google may process limited technical data such as IP address, device type and ad interactions under its own policies: How Google uses information from apps that use its services and AdMob and the Families policy.

Spoken instructions

The voice uses the text-to-speech engine already on the device. No audio is recorded or collected.

Parent area

Settings sit behind a parent gate. There are no in-app purchases and no links that take a child out of the app.

Children's privacy

The app is built to follow COPPA, GDPR-K and Google Play's Families policy. If you think a child's information was collected, contact us and we will help remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page and the date above will be updated.
